
## ZCode port layer

This repository additionally vendors the generated `plugins/pstack/` tree from [pstack-claude](https://github.com/michael-denyer/pstack-claude) and adapts it to ZCode. The ZCode port's modifications and additions (the `.zcode-plugin` manifest, `hooks/zcode-hooks.json`, `skills/poteto-mode/references/zcode-tools.md`, and the substitutions recorded in `tools/substitutions.json`) are (c) 2026 archeism and are released under the same [MIT license](LICENSE).
